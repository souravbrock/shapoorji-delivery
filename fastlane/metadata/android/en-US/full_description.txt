Shapoorji Delivery brings fresh vegetables, fruits, rice, pulses and daily essentials to your doorstep inside Shapoorji Housing Complex, Kolkata.

- Browse 47+ products across Fruits, Vegetables, Rice and Grocery
- Weight-based quantities (250g steps) with live pricing
- Cash / UPI on delivery, flat Rs.20 delivery fee
- Order tracking, PDF invoices and email receipts
- Works with the same account as the website

Note: delivery is currently available only inside Shapoorji Housing Complex. This app requires an internet connection and an account on spdelivery.reddevils.co.in.
